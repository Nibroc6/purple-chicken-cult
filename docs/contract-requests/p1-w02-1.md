# Contract request p1-w02-1: co-sign routes of the handshake, friend+ probation flag

**Status:** applied by the contract steward (additive) before the P1-W02 work commits.

**From:** P1-W02 · **Kind:** additive (two catalogue routes, one optional view field)
**Implementer:** P1-W02 (`gate/invites`, `api/invitesapi`) · **Readers:** web invites/join (P1-W02)

## Why

1. The invitee signup ceremony (fidelity #21, p1-w01-1 "Signup") splits `RegisterFinish`
   in two requests: `register/finish {credential}` verifies the new passkey and answers the
   get options over the Invite and Friendship drafts; a second request carries the get
   assertion and runs the RegisterFinish transaction, which aborts on a failed assertion.
   The catalogue has no route for that second request.
2. The signed-in side of a handshake co-signs too (`core.Cosign.Require`, "both sides of an
   invite", P1-W02 Consumes): the inviter signs the drafted Invite record, and both friends
   sign the friend+ Friendship of a Verified upgrade handshake. The intent comes from
   `POST /api/cosign {purpose, target: hs}` (P1-W01); the `{intent, assertion}` pair needs a
   route of the handshake that consumes the intent in the module's own transaction.
3. Phase 1 probation counts only **friend+** edges outside the own invite subtree (§8), while
   accounts mid-probation at the cut-over finish under the Phase 0 rule (fidelity #32). The
   invite screen must say which ("1 more friend+ outside your invite tree"); `ProbationView`
   cannot tell.
