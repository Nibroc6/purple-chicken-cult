# Contract request p1-w12-1: posts with media and the friend+ audience, hand-off (no contract change)

**Status:** open, for INT-1. Nothing for the steward: every interface used exists
(SCAFFOLD-1, p1-w04-1, p1-w04-2).

**From:** P1-W12 · **Kind:** none (integration notes) · **Owners of the follow-ups:** INT-1

## Provided by P1-W12

* **`gate/posts`:** `Draft.Media` (≤ 4 refs, `MaxMedia`) on `Create` and on every part of
  `CreateThread`. Media refs are opened with the other refs, before any Problem; then
  `too_many_media` (422); `feature_disabled` when `Deps.Media` is nil or media is off.
  `core.Media.Attach` runs in the post's transaction (per part), then `post_media` rows
  in order. Anything Attach refuses (another user's media, already attached, held,
  deleted, duplicate) is the uniform not-found and nothing is written.
* **Post record:** `media` lists the attached media ids (uuid strings) in order; edits
  re-sign the same list (media, like parents, is fixed at creation).
* **Audience `friend_plus`** is accepted (visibility already narrowed it); probation still
  forces `d1`.
* **`gate/views`:** `PostView.media` from `post_media` through `core.Media.Views` (only
  with `ViewOpts.WithBody`, so tree overflow rows stay cheap), and `PreviewView.image` from
  `link_previews.image_media_id` (written by P1-W11).
* **HTTP:** `POST /api/posts` `media?`, `POST /api/posts/thread` `parts[].media?`
  (`api/openapi/posts.yaml` `PostsMediaIn`).
* **Web:** the composer attaches up to 4 pictures through `MediaPicker` (removable thumbs;
  a thread puts them on its first part) and offers "Friend+ only"; Cards and Compact render
  `post.media` with `MediaImage` (`display` in the feed and the focal post, `thumb` for
  `variant="tree"`), preview images as thumbs, and `PostPreview` shows thumbs.

## Follow-ups

* **INT-1 journey:** upload → attach in the composer → friend sees `display` → unfriend →
  URL 404 (`web/e2e/p1-w12.spec.ts` covers deletion → 404, not unfriend).
* **Tree:** `TreeLayout` (P1-W08) gets thumbs automatically where it renders
  `PostLayout variant="tree"`; rows it draws itself show no media yet.
* **`/media/check`:** posts now carry media, so the standalone upload check page can go
  (P1-W04's call, noted in p1-w04-2).
* **Removed pictures:** removing a picture in the composer only drops it from the draft;
  the upload stays unattached and `media.process` deletes it after 24 h.
