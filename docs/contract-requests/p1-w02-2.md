## Provided by P1-W02

* **Protocol v2** (`internal/gate/invites/handshake.go` has the step list). The initiator's
  device posts E_i (`POST /invites/handshakes/{hs}/epub`), syncs to `GET …/frame` and renders
  `pcc2.<hs>.<E_i>.<n>.<mac>` every 250 ms from `HandshakeView.frame_key`. The claiming
  device claims with `{payload, e_v, sig_pub, proof}` and signs every later join call
  (`X-PCC-Join-Sig` over `"pcc-join-v1" ‖ hs ‖ sha256(payload) ‖ call`). SAS for every
  kind; `POST /join/audio/start` (claiming device) and `POST …/report` (initiator) score an
  attempt; at most five; dual QR via `POST /join/dual/frame`.
* **`core.Handshakes`** (gate/invites):
  * `OpenUpgrade(ctx, v, peer)` — v and peer plain friends, both active. The friend claims
    from their **own signed-in session** at `/join` ("Scan a friend's code"). Only a
    Verified outcome counts (Accepted reads as `retry` for upgrades); then both friends
    co-sign the friend+ Friendship (purpose `friend_upgrade`, `POST
    /invites/handshakes/{hs}/cosign`, the friend's own ref is `JoinStatus.hs`); the second
    signature writes it, calls `Edges.Upgrade`, updates graph memory and publishes
    `EdgeUpgraded`.
  * `OpenRecovery(ctx, guardian, account)` — the guardian is a friend+ of the account.
  * `ClaimRecovery(ctx, sess, payload)` — `payload` is the **JSON claim**
    (`invites.ClaimIn`: `{payload, e_v, sig_pub, proof}`); returns the account. Recovery
    handshakes cannot be claimed through `/join/claim`. The recovering device then runs the
    SAS and the sound through `/join/status` and `/join/audio/start` like an invitee
    (`web/src/features/join` handles `kind: "recovery"` with a short "continue" message).
  * `Result(ctx, hs)` — the latest outcome; a passed recovery handshake is `done`.
* **Web:** `web/src/features/invites/index.ts` exports `HandshakeHost` and `toOpenedVM`
  (render `<HandshakeHost opened={toOpenedVM(view)} />` with the `HandshakeView` your opener
  returns: it shows the frames, the SAS, listens, reports and co-signs). The device-side
  protocol is `features/invites/handshake.ts` (`ClaimDevice` for a claiming device).
* **Probation:** `users.probation_rule` (migration 3020): 0 = Phase 0 rule (accounts
  mid-probation at the cut-over), 1 = Phase 1 rule (30 days, three **friend+** edges outside
  the own invite subtree). `core.Probation.Reset` sets rule 1. `ProbationView.friend_plus`
  tells the invite screen which rule applies.
* **Diversity decay:** job `invites.diversity` (daily) sets `invite_budgets.diversity`.
