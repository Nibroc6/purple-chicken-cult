# Contract request p1-w10-2: guardian recovery and co-signed deletion (hand-off, no contract change)

**Status:** open, for INT-1. Nothing for the steward.

**From:** P1-W10 · **Kind:** none (integration notes) · **Owners of the follow-ups:** INT-1

## Provided by P1-W10

* **Guardian recovery** (`internal/gate/account/guardian.go` has the step list):
  * `POST /api/recovery/sessions {friend}` (guardian A, friend+ of the account, in person) →
    201 `HandshakeView`; opens `core.Handshakes.OpenRecovery` and an open guardian request
    (`recovery_requests.kind = 1`) with A's approval (`recovery_approvals.role = 1`). A may
    open it again (new code) until the handshake passed; anyone else gets `recovery_exists`.
  * `POST /api/recovery/claim` (guest session from `POST /api/join/start`) passes its JSON
    body to `ClaimRecovery` and answers **200 `{ticket}`** (the plan said 204). The device
    keeps the ticket in `localStorage` (`pcc.recovery.ticket`): guest sessions last 30
    minutes, the recovery a day or more.
  * `GET /api/recovery/status` takes the ticket in **`X-PCC-Recovery`** and answers
    `{state: claimed|approving|holding|released|cancelled|expired, release_at?, enroll_url?}`.
    Once released, every ask issues a fresh one-time enrollment URL (purpose recovery,
    usable from `release_at` for `EnrollTokenTTL`) and voids the previous one, so the plan's
    "`/api/enroll/*` accepts this session" became "the device gets the link". A hold that
    elapsed is released by this call when `recovery.release` has not run yet (as
    `GET /api/mod/recovery/{id}/link` does for manual recovery).
  * The request is **vouched** once `Handshakes.Result` is Accepted or Verified (first seen
    by any status or list call): the account's other friend+ friends get
    `recovery_approval` and see it in `GET /api/recovery/requests`.
  * `POST /api/recovery/requests/{id}/approve {intent, assertion}` (guardian B ≠ A ≠ the
    account, friend+ of it): co-signed `RecoveryApproval` record (parties: the account, A, B),
    the assertion also in `recovery_approvals.assertion`, 24 h hold, `friend_security`
    (`recovery_pending`) to every friend. `{id}` is a per-guardian ref
    (`Refs.Handshake(guardian, request id)`). Non-guardians: uniform 404; A:
    `second_guardian_required`.
  * Cancel, the banner and `recovery.release` are shared with manual recovery.
* **Co-signed deletion:** `POST /api/me/delete {confirm_handle, intent, assertion}` (purpose
  `account_delete`, `AccountDelete` record `{user, at}`); padded now (p1-w10-1).
* **Co-signed manual approval:** `POST /api/mod/recovery/{id}/approve {intent, assertion}`
  (purpose `recovery_approve`, `RecoveryApproval` with `method: moderators`).
* Sessions without a credential (test hooks) need no co-signature; their records are
  server-signed, as in friends and moderation.
* `GET /api/keys` adds `mine` (`Records.MyKeys`).

  every minute during the hold, under the anonymous `recovery` class (30/min per prefix).
