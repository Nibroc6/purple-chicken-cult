# Contract request p1-w10-1: record kinds for co-signed deletion and recovery, and the recovering device's steps

**Status:** applied by the contract steward before the P1-W10 work commits.

**From:** P1-W10 · **Kind:** additive (two record kinds, one padded route, exports) plus a
one-condition fix in P1-W02's handshake status · **Implementer:** contract steward ·
**Files:** `internal/gate/internal/core/interfaces.go`, `docs/IMPLEMENTATION.md`,
`internal/api/apix/pad/pad.go`, `internal/gate/invites/{handshake.go,upgrade_test.go}` (P1-W02),
`web/src/features/join/JoinPage.tsx`, `web/src/features/invites/{HandshakeHost.tsx,vm.ts}` (P1-W02)

## Why

1. **Record kinds.** P1-W10 co-signs account deletion and recovery approvals (plan §4.6,
   hand-off p1-w01-2: purposes `account_delete`, `recovery_approve`). A co-sign intent drafts
   a signed record of one `core.RecordKind`, and none of the nine kinds fits: a deletion is
   not a Revocation of one edge, and an approval is not a ModAction (guardians are not
   moderators). Two kinds are added; `records.Sign` accepts any kind with a name, the
   export and `VerifyExport` treat them like any other party record (they are not
   "high value" for the co-sign cut-over).
2. **Padding.** `POST /me/delete` now carries the co-sign `intent` (a ref) in its body, so
   `pad_test` requires it to be padded like `POST /auth/passkeys/finish`.
3. **The recovering device.** Guardian recovery (P1-W10) claims through
   `/api/recovery/claim`, never `/api/join/claim`, but its SAS and sound run through the
   join calls exactly as for an invitee. `features/recovery` reuses the join page's steps
   instead of copying them, so `Scan`, `Waiting`, `Sound` and `Dual` are exported.
4. **A done recovery handshake hid its outcome.** A recovery handshake turns `done` the
   moment its outcome passes (nothing is co-signed on it), and `status` returned the outcome
   only while `confirmed`, so the claiming device's `Sound` loop never saw it and stayed on
   "Play the sound" (`/join` and `/recover` alike). The status of a done recovery handshake
   now carries its outcome. P1-W02's recovery test checks it.
5. **The guardian's scan hint** named `/join`; a recovering device must open `/recover`.
