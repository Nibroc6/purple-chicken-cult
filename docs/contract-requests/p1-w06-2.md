## Provided by P1-W06

* **`core.ModQueue.Enqueue`** (`gate/moderation`) persists to `mod_queue` in the caller's
  transaction (`subject_media` included; p1-w04-2 done). Items of a signal source
  (`ModSrcSignalCluster`, `ModSrcStepUpRepeat`, `ModSrcInviteCluster`) with a subject merge
  into that subject's open item of the same source (summary replaced, the more urgent
  priority kept). Report items are written by `Report` itself (open P0 reports were
  backfilled by migration 3060).
* Queue (`GET /mod/queue`): one list by priority (csam 0, illegal 1, rest 2, step-up
  repeats 3); `ModItem.source`, `pending`, `claimed`, `csam` (`CSAMHolds.Hold` metadata,
  never content or a media URL); summary adds `subtree_confirmed`, `quarantine_k`,
  `inviter_marks`. `POST /mod/items/{id}/claim` (1 h).
* Two-person actions on an item: `suspend_user`, `dismiss` of a CSAM hold
  (`CSAMHolds.ReleaseAccount`), `csam_report` (`POST /mod/items/{id}/csam-report
  {report_ref}` → `CSAMHolds.Report` on confirmation), `quarantine_subtree`
  (`POST /mod/quarantine {subject_item, reason?}`). The second moderator confirms with
  `{intent, assertion}` from `POST /api/cosign {purpose: "mod_action", target: item id}`
  (purpose registered by `gate/moderation`; the signed `ModAction` record is stored with
  the assertion, `mod_actions.second_auth.record` is its hash). Sessions without a passkey
  (test hooks) keep the P0 fresh step-up.
* Quarantine: ≥ `PCC_QUARANTINE_K` subtree accounts whose suspension was confirmed since
  the root's last quarantine; `Probation.Reset(subtree \ root, +30 d)` and
  `InvitePenalties.Penalize(root, +30 d)` in the confirmation's transaction; nobody is
  notified (no new notification kind, so no `push.*` key is needed — P1-W05).
* Removed-account marks: written on a confirmed suspension, 90 days, audience = the removed
  account's component at that moment (roaring bitmap); `core.RemovedMarks.Marked` (a DB
  read per call) feeds `PersonView.invited_removed`.
* `gate/signals/behavior`: `signals.nightly` (cadence, MinHash/LSH similarity within
  three invite levels — bodies never stored —, invite clusters on `net_h`) and
  `signals.purge` (every `automation_signals` row after 30 days).
